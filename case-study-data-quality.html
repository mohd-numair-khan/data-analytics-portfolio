Environmental Reporting & Data Quality Platform

A reporting and data-quality concept designed to help users identify missing, incomplete, zero-reported and anomalous environmental submissions across a large global reporting scope.

The challenge

Large-scale environmental reporting can generate thousands of monthly submissions across many sites and KPIs. Manual review makes it difficult to consistently identify which reporting issues need attention first.

  • Missing monthly submissions
  • Explicit zero reporting
  • Incomplete required attributes
  • Potentially inconsistent values
  • Duplicate or anomalous records

The objective

Create one structured reporting approach that connects global monitoring with site-level investigation, so users can quickly move from “Where is the problem?” to “What exactly needs to be reviewed?”

The concept combines rule-based validation in Power BI with a scalable statistical data-quality prototype in Databricks.

Rule-based validation

The Power BI layer focuses on operational reporting logic. Records can be classified into intuitive states such as reported, zero-reported, incomplete or missing so users can distinguish different types of reporting behaviour.

This helps avoid treating every exception in the same way.

Statistical data-quality prototype

The Databricks/PySpark layer extends the concept beyond reporting completeness by adding missing-value checks, duplicate detection, statistical anomaly detection and risk scoring.

The result is a more scalable way to prioritize records or sites that deserve deeper investigation.

Business value

From manual checking to structured exception management

The main value of the solution is not simply a dashboard. It is the ability to systematically identify, classify and prioritize reporting-quality issues so users can focus their time on the cases that actually require investigation.
