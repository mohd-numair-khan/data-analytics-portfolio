CSRD Environmental Performance Dashboard

A structured Power BI reporting concept that brings climate, energy, water, wastewater and waste KPIs into one consistent management view for period-based analysis.

Confidentiality note: this portfolio case study uses synthetic values and recreated visuals. No original corporate dashboard, site information or employer data is shown.

The challenge

Environmental reporting spans several KPI families with different units, calculations and reporting logic. Without a consistent structure, users can spend too much time navigating separate views and interpreting how metrics relate to one another.

The objective

Create one coherent reporting interface where users can switch between environmental topics, compare periods, read values in the correct units and understand performance without rebuilding the report for every KPI family.

Reporting scope

The dashboard concept organizes related disclosures into clear topic blocks rather than presenting all environmental KPIs in one crowded view.

Climate & GHGScope 1, Scope 2, reduction metrics and related performance indicators.
EnergyTotal energy consumption, fossil, renewable and related share metrics.
Water & WastewaterWithdrawal, consumption, reused/recycled water and wastewater discharge.
WasteWaste generation, hazardous/non-hazardous categories and disposal pathways.
Period ComparisonStructured year and month analysis with consistent KPI presentation.
Management ViewClear, concise presentation focused on decision-relevant trends and exceptions.

KPI modelling

The reporting logic uses structured KPI definitions so that different disclosures can be routed through consistent calculations while preserving the correct units and display rules.

This makes the dashboard easier to maintain as the number of metrics grows.

User experience

Topic-based navigation reduces clutter. Instead of forcing users through one oversized matrix, each environmental block can be surfaced through clear controls while keeping the overall visual language consistent.

The result is a more readable and decision-friendly report.

Business value

One reporting structure for multiple environmental KPI families

The value of the solution is the consistency it brings to environmental reporting. Users can navigate different KPI families through one coherent interface, reducing fragmentation and making performance trends easier to interpret.
