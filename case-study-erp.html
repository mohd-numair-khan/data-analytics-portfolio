ERP & Business Performance Analytics

A reporting automation case study showing how SQL, Excel, Power Query and VBA can turn recurring ERP extracts into reliable KPI reporting for sales, operations and business reviews.

The challenge

Recurring weekly and monthly reporting can become time-consuming when large ERP extracts must be validated, reshaped and manually updated before business review meetings.

The objective

Standardize the workflow from raw ERP data to validated KPI outputs, reduce repetitive manual work and improve consistency across recurring business reports.
