Case Study 03 · Executive Reporting · KPI Analytics

Management Performance Commentary Dashboard

A Power BI management reporting concept that combines KPI movement with executive commentary, status, key drivers and key challenges—so decision-makers can understand both what changed and why.

Confidentiality note: all divisions, values and commentary below are synthetic. No original employer screenshot or internal performance data is used.

The challenge

Traditional KPI dashboards can show whether a metric moved up or down, but senior users often still need context: Is the movement good? What drove it? What should be watched next?

The objective

Create a concise management layer that connects current performance, prior-period comparison, target direction and narrative interpretation in one readable view.

Executive summary

CO₂e performance improved versus the previous period, supported by stronger energy efficiency and a higher renewable electricity contribution. Performance remains close to the target trajectory.

Key drivers

Energy efficiency improvement; higher renewable electricity share; lower energy demand.

Key challenges

A small number of high-energy sites remain above the desired performance trajectory.

Analytical logic

The dashboard compares selected KPI performance with a prior reporting period and applies consistent status logic to distinguish improving, stable and declining trends.

This ensures the narrative is anchored in the underlying KPI movement rather than presented as a disconnected text box.

User experience

Topic and division controls allow users to switch context without navigating multiple report pages. The visual hierarchy puts the most decision-relevant information first: status, summary, drivers and challenges.

What I contributed

Business design

  • Structured executive summary, commentary, drivers and challenges
  • Designed topic/division navigation
  • Aligned KPI movement with management interpretation
  • Improved readability for non-technical users

Technical implementation

  • DAX measures for current and previous performance

  • Dynamic text measures
  • Power BI visual and interaction design
Business value

A dashboard that explains performance—not just displays it

The solution reduces the interpretation gap between analytical outputs and management decisions by connecting numbers with the context users need to understand performance quickly.
